`licenses/pdfium/` (copied unchanged from the PDFium release archive, see §2.1).

  (`licenses\`).

  the output are distributable. Whoever distributes Mavue binaries must add significant primary functionality, require
  distributors and end users to agree to terms that protect the code and Microsoft at least as much as Microsoft's
